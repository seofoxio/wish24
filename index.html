01. Problem & Opportunity

What We Understand

Indians celebrate often and gift in groups, but the tools today are scattered and awkward.

⚠️

The Problem

  • Duplicate & Unwanted Gifts: Hosts get 3 pressure cookers, 5 wall clocks, or unneeded decor.
  • Guest Confusion: Guests stress over "What should I buy?" or "Will they like it?"
  • Messy Money Chasing: Friends pooling on WhatsApp turns into awkward reminders.
  • Cash Envelopes: Traditional cash envelopes leave no record and zero transparency.
💡

The Core Insight

Every single celebration already requires an invitation. If the gift wishlist & contribution link rides directly inside the digital invitation, asking for what you actually want stops feeling awkward!

🧩

What Exists Today

Invitation apps (like Canva/WhatsApp images) do invitations only. Registry apps (Kiki, Gifaa, Hazlnut) do gifting only, mostly tailored strictly to weddings. Nobody in India owns both in ONE seamless link.

⚡

Why Now?

  • UPI Infrastructure: Instant, zero-fee micro-contributions are standard for 300M+ Indians.
  • Digital Invites Normalised: WhatsApp invites have completely replaced paper cards for modern hosts.
  • Category Momentum: Indian registry apps launching in 2025–26 prove consumer habit formation is starting now.
🎯

Our Answer: The Wish24 Link

One unified Wish24 link: Invitation + RSVP + Wishlist + Group Contributions for EVERY occasion, complete with a venue QR code for guests at the function itself!

04. Product Architecture

What Wish24 Is & How It Works

A digital invitation & group gifting platform for any occasion, built specifically for India.

The Core Concept

A host creates an event, picks a beautiful template, adds the items they actually want, and shares a single link: wish24.in/rahul.

Guests view the invitation details, RSVP, scroll to the wishlist, and chip in any amount directly via UPI to fund gifts, experiences, or a cash fund.

CORE PROMISE
  • No duplicate or unwanted gifts
  • No awkward "what do you want?"
  • No chasing friends for money on WhatsApp

V1 Payment Architecture: Direct UPI to Host

Money moves directly to the host's personal UPI ID. Wish24 acts purely as the record-keeper and progress logger. This completely eliminates regulatory payment-licence and escrow complexities at launch. On-platform payment processing & escrow wallet collection will be introduced in V2 after legal/tax compliance framework from CA/lawyer.
